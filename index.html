✦ FAQ

Questions customers ask us

What does BUTOTAM Rentals Services rent out in Ibadan?

We rent canopies, plain plastic chairs, square tables, plates, cutlery, coolers and other party essentials, subject to current stock. These items can support weddings, birthdays, funerals, church programmes, corporate events and private functions. Call or WhatsApp +234 803 809 0208 to ask about your requirements.

Where is BUTOTAM Rentals Services located in Ibadan?

We are at No 32, Aroro, Makinde Road, Ojoo, Ibadan 200134, Oyo State. Delivery to Moniya, University of Ibadan (UI), Agbowo, Arulogun Road and nearby areas can be discussed and confirmed for your venue.

Do you deliver and set up rented items within Ibadan?

Delivery and setup can be arranged for some locations, subject to confirmation. Delivery fees and setup arrangements depend on the event location and order. Share your venue and event date so we can confirm.

How much does it cost to rent event equipment in Ibadan?

Prices depend on the items, quantity, rental duration and delivery location. Call or WhatsApp us with your event date and requirements for a quote.

How early should I book a rental for my event in Ibadan?

Contact us as early as possible, especially for weekends and busy festive periods, so we can check stock and discuss your requirements. Items are not reserved until your booking is confirmed.

Do I need to pay a deposit to reserve my rental?

An 80% advance payment is required to confirm a booking, with the remaining 20% due before delivery on the event day. A caution fee also applies. Ask us about the fee amount and its refund conditions before confirming.

What happens if a rented item is damaged or lost?

Customers are responsible for rented items while they are in their care. Items are checked at handover and return. Ask us before booking how the caution fee applies and what charges may apply if an item is damaged or lost.

How long is the rental period, and can I extend it?

The rental period and return time are agreed during booking. Extensions may be possible by arrangement, subject to availability; confirm any additional charges with us in advance.

Are you open on weekends and public holidays?

Yes. BUTOTAM Rentals Services is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including weekends and public holidays.

How do I book BUTOTAM Rentals Services?

Call or WhatsApp +234 803 809 0208. Share your event date, location, requested items and quantities. We will confirm availability and price. Your booking is confirmed once the 80% advance payment is made.
